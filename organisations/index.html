01 · identity

Your logo and colours, checked for you.

Upload a logo and pick a colour. loft generates the tints, checks text contrast, and applies it to every screen, email and printable document.

02 · your link

Your organisation gets its own address on loft. Anyone who opens it sees your sign-in page, your colours and your name.

  • tanziro.com/spaces/your-name, chosen by your admin

  • rename it any time; the old link forwards

branding switched on → admin picks the name → share the link

custom domains aren't offered. your link lives on tanziro.com, so there's no DNS or certificate to manage.

questions

Before you email

Why email instead of a checkout?
There's no billing system to sign up for. You get a normal invoice, pay it, and branding is switched on.
Can we see it before paying?
Yes. Any admin can set up the brand in Brand studio and preview it. It goes live once branding is switched on.
Can we use our own domain?
No. Your branded link is tanziro.com/spaces/your-name. It needs no setup from anyone.
Can we pay by bank transfer?
Yes. The invoice has a card payment link and bank details, in USD.
What if we stop paying?
Admins see a reminder before the paid period ends, then get 14 days' grace. After that your spaces return to the standard look. Nothing is deleted.
Do you take a cut of what our hosts invoice?
No. Invoices use the hosts' own payment links. loft never touches the money.
