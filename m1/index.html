01 · rooms

Open a room. Anyone joins with a link.

A board, chat and audio in one tab. Share the link anywhere, and clients, teammates or students join from a phone without making an account.

  • live cursors with names, so you see who is writing where
  • follow-me and lock keep everyone on your page
  • guest join on: they walk in. off: you let each one in
  • your own Meet or Zoom link still works as a fallback
workflow

start room → copy link → paste in WhatsApp → guest taps it → you tap let in → same page, same voice

vs Meet: Meet shares your screen. loft shares the page, and everyone can write on it.

02 · diagrams

Draw it by hand. Or type it.

A whiteboard that behaves like a diagram tool. Flowchart shapes, connectors that snap and re-route, sections and sticky notes. Or type a few lines and get a tidy diagram you can still drag around.

  • connectors snap to shapes and follow them when moved: straight, elbow or curved
  • smoothed pen strokes; a rough box or circle becomes a clean shape
  • diagram as code for flowcharts, sequence diagrams, ERDs and swimlanes
  • workshop kit: dot voting, hide-and-reveal, shared timer, cursor chat
how it works

sketch or type → auto-layout → tidy up → present or export as PNG / PDF

it all runs in your browser: no plugin, nothing to install, nothing extra on the server.

03 · files

Drop a file. Give everyone their own copy.

PDFs, photos and slide decks become pages on the board. Your browser does the converting, so it runs on a $3 host and phones only load light images.

  • PDFs and photos turn into pages you can mark up
  • paste a public Google Slides or Docs link to import it
  • hand out: everyone gets a private copy, you see them all
  • the marked-up board is saved as a PDF in the space's files
workflow

drop brief.pdf → pages appear → hand out → each person works on a copy → copies come back to you

vs Miro: a great board. loft's board knows who is in the space and turns the page into follow-up work.

04 · calls

Audio that costs nothing. Video when it earns it.

Calls are built in and audio comes first. Up to 6 people talk peer-to-peer for free. Groups of up to 12 use stage mode: one person speaks, the rest raise a hand.

  • peer-to-peer audio for up to 6 talking, free
  • stage mode: hands up, the host gives a 60-second turn
  • bigger event? use your Zoom or Meet link for the call
  • a daily budget steps video down to audio, never cuts a session
workflow

hand up → host taps let them speak → 60 s on the floor → mic goes back

vs Zoom: Zoom is a call with a whiteboard attached. loft is a workspace with a call attached.

05 · hallway

See who's around. Pull them into a room.

A Slack-style side panel shows live rooms, your spaces as channels, and direct messages with who is online. Talking and working happen in the same place.

  • live rooms with one-click join, or start a new one
  • knock on anyone who is free; they land on your board
  • room invites arrive as cards in the chat
  • clients and guests chat free, only in their own spaces
workflow

Leena is free → knock → she taps join → you're both on the board, audio on

vs Slack: Slack is where work gets talked about. In loft the talk sits next to the board, the session and the bill.

06 · spaces

A stream people actually read.

Every space, whether a client, a class or a team, gets familiar tabs: Stream, Chat, Work, Files, People and Progress. The difference is what lands in the stream on its own.

  • recaps post themselves after each session, with the board as a PDF
  • must-acknowledge posts show exactly who hasn't seen them
  • files sorted by topic, with who opened what
  • tasks, quizzes and sign-offs, joined with a code or link
workflow

end session → recap, board PDF and follow-ups post to the stream → everyone in the space sees it

vs Google Classroom: Classroom stops at the assignment. loft also runs the live session and sends the invoice.

07 · agendas

Write the agenda once. It runs the session.

A plan has timed sections. In the room they become the agenda timer, the pages on the board, the hand-outs, the closing check and the follow-up work.

  • a pacing bar shows where the hour goes
  • attach a plan to a session and the room is ready
  • templates for lessons, sprints, retros and client reviews
  • optional AI with your own key drafts the first version
workflow

agenda → attach to Monday 16:00 → start room → timer, pages and hand-outs are already there

vs docs and AI planners: they write good plans. loft's plan becomes the live agenda, the board and the follow-up.

08 · money

Invoices that explain themselves.

loft counts the sessions that happened and drafts the invoice. Every line links to its recap, and your payment link is attached for you, so you never paste it again.

  • one default link, a link per payer, or a {amount} template
  • per session, monthly, packages, retainers and fixed fees
  • payers tap “I've paid” and you confirm with one tap
  • reminders 3 days before and after the due date, in USD
workflow

session ends → line added → invoice on the 1st → client pays with your link → taps I've paid → you confirm → receipt

vs invoicing apps: they bill well but never see the work. loft's invoice shows what each line paid for.

09 · insights

Know who is drifting before they leave.

Last seen, last login, attendance, open tasks and payments for everyone in a space, with the people at risk at the top and a nudge one tap away.

  • reminders go out 24 hours before something is due
  • missed two sessions? the right person hears about it
  • write a custom nudge to a group and schedule it
  • in-app and push first, email or a WhatsApp link as backup
workflow

3 haven't replied → custom nudge → Friday 18:00 → 2 respond by Saturday

vs spreadsheets: a sheet tells you who paid. loft tells you who is drifting and lets you act on it.

10 · checks

Quick checks with integrity signals. No webcam.

Quizzes, polls and sign-off checks grade themselves. For checks that matter, a 6 KB script records tab switches, pasting and leaving fullscreen, and gives you a score to review, not a verdict.

  • multiple choice, true/false and short answers, auto-graded
  • time limits and attempts enforced on the server
  • an event timeline per attempt, flagged for a human look
  • no camera, no microphone, nothing to install
workflow

switches tab at 04:12 → event logged → score 78 → you review the timeline

vs proctoring suites: they watch the person. loft watches the browser, and leaves the judgement to you.

11 · phones

Built for a phone on patchy data.

People join from a phone, audio-only if the network is weak. Pages arrive as light images, the app installs from the browser, and every screen works one-handed.

  • one link to join, no app store, installs as an app
  • audio-only mode for slow connections
  • raise a hand, follow the page, keep private notes
  • clients pay and check progress from the same phone
workflow

tap the link → audio connects → follow the board → raise a hand

vs Meet on a phone: a shared screen is too small to read. loft sends the page itself, so it fits.

Questions

  • Is it really free?

    Yes, for every host, member and guest. Schools and organisations pay only if they want their own logo, colours and name on it.

  • Do I still need Zoom or Meet?

    No. Audio is built in and free for small groups. Keep your own link as a fallback, or switch on video when you need it.

  • Can people join without an account?

    Yes, from a link. Turn guest join off and you'll be asked to let each person in.

  • How do I get paid?

    Through your own payment link: Stripe, PayPal, Wise or any URL. loft never touches the money; it drafts the invoice and keeps track.

  • Where is my data?

    At tanziro.com/spaces, in one database on our server. Export everything any time.

  • What can I run on it?

    Client calls, workshops, classes, coaching and team rituals. Each space picks a mode, which only changes the labels and templates.

  • How many people can join?

    Up to 12 in a room at once, and calls are smoothest with up to 6 talking. loft is made for small teams and small groups, not webinars.

  • What if a session is bigger?

    Use your Zoom or Meet link for the call and keep loft for the board, the plan and the invoice. Organisations can also add their own video provider.
